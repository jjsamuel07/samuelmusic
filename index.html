Pianist · New York City

Elegant live piano for the moments that matter.

I am a New York City-based pianist providing elegant live music for weddings, baptisms, birthday parties, church ceremonies, proposals and private gatherings. Every song is chosen with you and rehearsed in advance.

My repertoire spans the classical concert literature of the great composers, traditional wedding pieces, modern romantic songs, and Christian and worship music upon request. Piano tutoring is available as well.

01 What to
expect

Every detail agreed long before the first guest arrives.

From the moment a date is held, we stay in close communication to plan every musical detail of your event — a wedding, a baptism, a birthday or a church service alike. Virtual consultations cover your preferred style, specific song requests, and the atmosphere you want in the room.

We then map the moments that matter and the music that carries them: the prelude as guests arrive, a processional or entrance, hymns and readings, a toast or a cake, and the recessional or closing set — so every transition feels seamless and intentional. If you have a family member or guest who would like to perform a vocal piece, I can also provide live piano accompaniment.
